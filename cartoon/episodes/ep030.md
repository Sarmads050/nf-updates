TITLE: Paanch Kilo Matar!
DURATION: ~115 s
HOOK: AMMI — Daniyal! Paanch kilo matar? Main ne aadha kilo kaha tha!
CHARACTERS: AMMI, DANIYAL, ABBA, MEHAK, SANA
LOCATION: Aangan (subah), Drawing room (shaam), Kitchen (raat)

SCENES:

## Scene 1 — Aangan • Subah
AMMI: Daniyal! Paanch kilo matar? Main ne aadha kilo kaha tha!
NARRATOR: Sardiyon mein matar saste ho jate hain... aur Daniyal ko sasti deal ka bohot shauq hai!
DANIYAL: Ammi, sabzi wale ne kaha, paanch kilo loge to bhaav kam hoga. Deal meri!
AMMI: Deal tumhari... to ab yeh paanch kilo chheelega kaun?
DANIYAL: Objection, my lord! Matar chheelna mere contract mein nahi tha!
ABBA: Humare zamane mein poora ghar mil kar matar chheelta tha... bina shikayat ke!
MEHAK: Phir to aaj sab se pehle aap chheelenge na, Abba?   <-- JOKE
ABBA: Hanji... hanji. Pehle main apni chai to khatam kar loon.

## Scene 2 — Drawing Room • Shaam
NARRATOR: Shaam tak poora ghar matar chheelne baith gaya.
AMMI: Daniyal, tumhari plate mein matar kam aur chhilke zyada kyun hain?
DANIYAL: Ammi, main quality check kar raha hoon. Har dana mazedaar hai!
MEHAK: Ammi, Daniyal bhai pachaas daane kha chuke hain. Main gin rahi thi!
DANIYAL: Objection! Is gawah ki ginti par bharosa nahi kiya ja sakta!
MEHAK: Saboot bhi hai... aap ke moonh mein abhi bhi matar hain!   <-- JOKE
AMMI: Aur aap? Humare zamane wale haath kyun ruk gaye?
ABBA: Humare zamane mein matar bare bare hote thay... jaldi chhil jate thay!   <-- JOKE
AMMI: Main ne pehle hi kaha tha! Paanch kilo ek din mein khatam nahi honge!

## Scene 3 — Kitchen • Raat
SANA: Ammi, pareshan na hon. Bache hue matar hum freezer mein rakh lenge.
AMMI: Freezer mein? Kharab to nahi ho jayenge, Sana?
SANA: Nahi Ammi. Halka sa ubaal kar, thanda kar ke shopper mein band karein. Mahinon chalenge.
AMMI: Waah, meri M.A. pass bahu! Phir to har hafte matar pulao banega.
DANIYAL: Dekha Ammi? Meri deal ne poore mahine ka intezam kar diya!
AMMI: Deal tumhari thi, mehnat hum sab ki. Kal se sabzi Mehak laye gi.
DANIYAL: Lekin Ammi, sabzi wala keh raha tha... das kilo par aur sasta!
AMMI: Daniyal! Ek lafz aur... aur woh das kilo tum akele chheeloge!   <-- JOKE
SANA: Mil kar kaam karo... to paanch kilo bhi halke lagte hain.

JOKE: Mehak — 'sab se pehle aap chheelenge na, Abba?'; Mehak — 'aap ke moonh mein abhi bhi matar hain!'; Abba — 'humare zamane mein matar bare bare hote thay'; Ammi — 'das kilo tum akele chheeloge!'
ENDING: Sana's freezer idea saves the extra peas; Ammi gives vegetable duty to Mehak; Sana: mil kar kaam karo to paanch kilo bhi halke lagte hain.
LOGIC: blanch-cool-freeze is a real way to keep peas for months; Abba finishes his chai before shelling; peas shelled in evening, frozen at night.
