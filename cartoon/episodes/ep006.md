SCENE 1 — Drawing Room • Dopehar
NARRATOR: Shaadiyon ka season shuru ho gaya hai... aur aaj Nawabshah ke is ghar mein aaya hai ek shaadi ka card!
MEHAK: Ammi! Aap ke phone par Nimra Baji ne shaadi ka card bheja hai!
MEHAK: Nimra Baji ki nand ki shaadi hai. Agle hafte Karachi mein... teen function hain!
AMMI: Haye! Agle hafte? Aur mere paas pehenne ke liye koi naya jora nahi hai!
MEHAK: Ammi, mujhe bhi naye jore chahiyein... mehndi, baraat aur walima ke liye!

SCENE 2 — Aangan • Shaam

SANA: Aap ka maroon jora sirf ek dafa pehna gaya hai. Naya dupatta lagayein, bilkul naya lagega!
MEHAK: Aur mera kya, Bhabi? Main shaadi mein kya pehnoongi?

IMTIAZ: Ni hao, sab ko! Main ne suna hai, Karachi mein shaadi hai?
AMMI: Haan, Imtiaz! Aur dulhan ke tohfe ke liye paise bhi chahiye honge.
IMTIAZ (JOKE): Ammi, salary agle hafte aayegi. Abhi mere batwe mein sirf mei you hai... yaani kuch nahi!

ABBA: To phir, begum? Kitne naye joron ka bill banega?

MEHAK: Aur Abba ka bhoora kurta? Woh bhi phir se chalega?

ABBA: Lekin tumhare batwe mein to mei you tha!
IMTIAZ (JOKE): Cash on delivery, Abba! Tab tak salary bhi aa jayegi!
