SCENE 1 — Drawing Room • Dopehar
NARRATOR: Sardiyan shuru ho gayi hain... lekin aaj Nawabshah ke is ghar mein aaya hai pichhle garam mahine ka bijli ka bill!
ABBA: Yeh kya? Is mahine ka bill pichhle mahine se dugna hai!
AMMI: Dugna? Main to din bhar sirf ek pankha chalati hoon!
ABBA: To phir yeh bijli kaun kha raha hai? Sab ko bulao, abhi!
DANIYAL: Abba, aap ne bulaya? Kya ghar mein aaj adalat lag rahi hai?
ABBA: Haan! Bill dugna aaya hai. Batao, itni bijli kis ne kharch ki?
DANIYAL (JOKE): Objection, Abba! Saboot ke baghair kisi ko mujrim nahi keh sakte!
AMMI: To jao, saboot dhoondo! Faraz ko bhi saath le jao.

SCENE 2 — Larkon Ka Kamra • Shaam
NARRATOR: Shaam ko dono bhai pahunche ghar ke sab se thande kamre mein...
FARAZ: Daniyal, ghar mein sab se zyada bijli AC khata hai... aur AC sirf is kamre mein hai!
DANIYAL: Yani mulzim hai... Arsalan!
ARSALAN: Main? Main to sirf parhai kar raha tha... computer par!
FARAZ: Parhai? Computer par to cricket ka game chal raha hai!
DANIYAL: Aur yeh dekho! AC ke remote par abhi tak sixteen likha hai!
ARSALAN (JOKE): Ada, garmi mein dimagh garam ho jaye, to parhai kaise hogi?
FARAZ: Aur yeh teen charger plug mein lage hain, lekin phone ek bhi nahi!
ARSALAN: Woh to main ne emergency ke liye ready rakhe hain!
DANIYAL (JOKE): Your honour, mulzim ka bayan record ho gaya. Case bilkul pakka hai!

SCENE 3 — Drawing Room • Raat
FARAZ: Abba, saboot mil gaya. AC sixteen par chalta raha, aur charger din raat lage rahe.
ABBA: Arsalan! Humare zamane mein garmi mein haath ka pankha hota tha!
ARSALAN: Sorry, Abba. Agli garmi mein AC sirf raat ko, aur chhabbees par chalaunga.
FARAZ: Aur jo cheez istemal na ho, uska plug nikal do. Bill khud kam ho jayega.
AMMI: Aur dopehar mein sab ek kamre mein baithenge. Ek pankha, poora khandan!
ABBA: Shabash! Aur agle mahine ka bill tum check karoge, Arsalan.
ARSALAN: Theek hai, Abba! Lekin bill kam aaya, to bache paison se mera naya game?
AMMI (JOKE): Bilkul! Bache paison se tumhare liye naya haath ka pankha aayega!
ABBA: Waah, begum! Ab yeh bill bhi sasta, aur pankha bhi!
