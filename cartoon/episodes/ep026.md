TITLE: Hont Ka Mukadma!
DURATION: ~110 s
HOOK: NARRATOR: Nawabshah mein thandi khushk hawa chal rahi hai... aur Daniyal ke hont phat gaye hain!
CHARACTERS: Daniyal, Abba, Mehak, Ammi, Sana
LOCATION: Nawabshah drawing room (subah), kitchen (dopahar), drawing room (shaam)

SCENE 1: Drawing Room • Subah
- NARRATOR: Nawabshah mein thandi khushk hawa chal rahi hai... aur Daniyal ke hont phat gaye hain!
- DANIYAL: Objection, my lord! Mera muakkil bekasoor hai... aah!
- MEHAK: Kya hua, Ada Daniyal? Kal ke moot court se pehle hi case haar gaye?
- DANIYAL: Mehak, muskurata hoon to hont phat-te hain. Main judge ke saamne hans bhi nahi sakta!
- ABBA: Humare zamane mein hum sardi mein bhi paani khoob peete thay. Tum kitna peete ho?
- DANIYAL: Abba, din mein do glass... aur dono chai ke!   [JOKE]
- ABBA: Chai paani nahi hoti, beta! Jao, Ammi se ilaaj poocho.

SCENE 2: Kitchen • Dopahar
- NARRATOR: Ilaaj ke liye Daniyal seedha kitchen pohancha.
- DANIYAL: Ammi, mere hont dekhein! Kal moot court hai, koi ilaaj batayein.
- AMMI: Aasaan totka hai! Raat ko naaf mein do boond sarson ka tel daal lena.
- DANIYAL: Objection! Hont upar hain, aur tel neeche? Yeh kaun sa qanoon hai?
- AMMI: Yeh Ammi ka qanoon hai, beta. Is ki koi appeal nahi hoti!   [JOKE]
- SANA: Ammi theek keh rahi hain. Aur yeh lo, pehle ek glass paani piyo.
- DANIYAL: Shukriya, Bhabi. Lekin hont sookh jate hain, to main zabaan pherta rehta hoon.
- SANA: Isi liye to phat-te hain! Honton par zabaan mat pherein, vaseline lagayein.
- AMMI: Aur sardi mein bhi din mein aath glass paani. Chai ginti mein nahi aati!
- DANIYAL: Theek hai, Ammi. Aaj se paani mera sab se bara gawah hoga!

SCENE 3: Drawing Room • Shaam
- NARRATOR: Shaam tak Daniyal ne paani piya, vaseline lagayi... aur zabaan ko chhutti de di!
- DANIYAL: Objection, my lord! Dekhiye, ab main muskura bhi sakta hoon!
- MEHAK: Waah Ada! Lekin aaj aap ne kitne glass paani piya?
- DANIYAL: Poore aath! Ab main wakeel kam, paani ki tanki zyada lag raha hoon!   [JOKE]
- AMMI: Aur raat ko naaf mein tel daalna mat bhoolna, wakeel sahab!
- DANIYAL: Ji Ammi! Ammi ki adalat mein... case closed!
- ABBA: Shabash! Sardi mein bhi paani peete raho. Sehat ka yehi asal raaz hai!

JOKE / PUNCHLINE: 'Do glass... dono chai ke!' ; 'Ammi ka qanoon — is ki koi appeal nahi hoti!' ; 'wakeel kam, paani ki tanki zyada'
ENDING: Daniyal can smile again; Abba: sardi mein bhi paani peete raho.
