TITLE: Mehman Aa Rahe Hain!
DURATION: ~55 seconds
HOOK: Phone ki ghanti — Ammi chillati hain "Nimra apne sasural walon ke saath aadhe ghante mein aa rahi hai?!"
CHARACTERS: Ammi, Bhabi Sana, Mehak, Ada Imtiaz, Nimra (phone par)
LOCATION: Nawabshah kitchen, shaam

SCENE 1
(Phone bajta hai. Ammi phone uthati hain)
AMMI: Kya?! Nimra apne sasural walon ke saath aadhe ghante mein aa rahi hai?!
NIMRA (phone par): Haan Ammi! Bas chai aur kuch halka sa... zyada takalluf mat karna!
AMMI: Halka sa? Sasural wale hain! Ghar mein na samosay, na mithai!
SANA: Ammi, ghabraiye mat. Aloo aur besan hai — pakoray aur chutney das minute mein!
AMMI: Theek hai, lekin chai meri banegi. Tumhari wali pheeki chai nahi!
(Ada Imtiaz thaka hua andar aata hai)
IMTIAZ: Kya hua? Itna shor? Lagta hai koi VIP aa raha hai!
MEHAK: Ada, Nimra Baji ke sasural wale! Aap jaldi se mithai le aayein!
IMTIAZ (JOKE): Hao de! Chinese mein matlab "zaroor"... lekin batua dekha to — mei you! Yaani paise nahi!
AMMI: Yeh lo Chinese sahab, paise! Aur jaldi wapas aana!
(Ada bahar jata hai. Pakoray tayyar)
SANA: Ammi, pakoray tayyar! Aur aapki chai ki khushbu poore ghar mein!
AMMI: Shabash beti. Tumhare pakoray, meri chai — aaj to sab khush ho jayenge!
(Notification ki awaz)
MEHAK: Ammi... Nimra Baji ka message: "Traffic mein phans gaye, kal aayenge!"
(Ada mithai ka thaila le kar wapas)
IMTIAZ (PUNCHLINE): Koi baat nahi! Pakoray thanday hon, is se pehle... hum hi kha lete hain!

JOKE: Imtiaz ke Chinese alfaaz "Hao de" (zaroor) aur "Mei you" (nahi hai) — aur aakhir mein mehman kal aayenge, pakoray ghar wale khud kha jaate hain.
ENDING: Mil kar kaam karo to mushkil bhi aasan — aur ghar walon ke saath khana sab se mazedaar.
