SCENES:

## Drawing Room • Subah
AMMI: Arsalan! Phone neeche rakho! Aaj ghar mein safai ka din hai!
NARRATOR: Nawabshah mein shaadiyon ka mausam hai... aur kal ghar mehmaan aa rahe hain!
ARSALAN: Ammi, bas ek over aur! Phir pakka safai karunga.
AMMI: Over nahi, jhaaroo! Tum aur Daniyal apna kamra saaf karo.
ABBA: Theek hai, bachay safai karein... main supervisor hoon.
MEHAK: Abba, supervisor sahab akhbar ke peeche se kaise dekhenge?
ABBA: Humare zamane mein supervisor sirf awaaz se kaam karwata tha!
AMMI: To phir awaaz ke saath... sofa ke neeche bhi jhaaroo laga dijiye!   <-- JOKE

## Larkon Ka Kamra • Dopahar
NARRATOR: Udhar larkon ke kamre mein... bed ke neeche se khazana nikalne laga!
ARSALAN: Ada! Mera purana bat mil gaya! Do saal se gayab tha!
DANIYAL: Aur yeh dekho... meri pehli qanoon ki kitaab!
DANIYAL: Ek minute... bas ek page parh leta hoon.
NARRATOR: Daniyal parhta raha, Arsalan bat ghumata raha... aur ek minute aadha ghanta ban gaya!
AMMI: Yeh safai ho rahi hai... ya museum khul gaya hai?   <-- JOKE
DANIYAL: Objection, my lord! Yeh safai ka hissa hai... cheezon ki jaanch!
AMMI: Objection overruled! Kitaab shelf par, aur jhaaroo haath mein!
ARSALAN: Ammi, main to sach mein kaam kar raha tha!
AMMI: Haan... bat ghuma kar hawa saaf kar rahe thay!   <-- JOKE

## Drawing Room • Shaam
NARRATOR: Shaam tak saare kamre chamak gaye... lekin supervisor sahab?
MEHAK: Abba! Aap sofa ke neeche wala purana akhbar parh rahe hain?
ABBA: Beta, yeh paanch saal purana hai. Us waqt ki khabrein bhi zaroori hain!
MEHAK: Ammi ko bata doon?   <-- JOKE
AMMI: Main ne pehle hi kaha tha! Safai mein sab se pehle supervisor hi gum hota hai!   <-- JOKE
ABBA: Achha baba... yeh akhbar raddi mein. Aur kal mehmaanon ki chai main banaunga!
AMMI: Purani cheezon mein yaadein hoti hain... bas pehle safai, phir yaadein!
MEHAK: Aur kal supervisor sahab ki chai... sab ke saamne pakki!   <-- JOKE

JOKE: 'Safai ho rahi hai ya museum khul gaya hai?' + 'Ammi ko bata doon?' + 'Safai mein sab se pehle supervisor hi gum hota hai!'
ENDING: Abba gives in, will make tea for the guests; pehle safai, phir yaadein.
