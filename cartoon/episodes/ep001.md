TITLE: WiFi Ka Mujrim Kaun?
DURATION: ~60 seconds
HOOK: Arsalan chillata hai "Ada Faraz! WiFi phir band ho gaya!"
CHARACTERS: Arsalan, Abba, Mehak, Faraz, Daniyal
LOCATION: Nawabshah drawing room, shaam

SCENE 1
ARSALAN: Ada Faraz! WiFi phir band ho gaya! Mera match beech mein atak gaya!
MEHAK: Match nahi, game atka hai. Saara din mobile pe!
ABBA: Humare zamane main to WiFi nahi tha, phir bhi hum khush thay!
(Faraz andar aata hai)
FARAZ: Hanji, hanji, aa gaya tech support. Router ki light laal hai... kisi ne taar nikaali hai!
(Daniyal andar aata hai)
DANIYAL: Objection! Jab tak saboot na mile, har shakhs begunah hai!
FARAZ: Saboot yeh raha. Router ka plug nikaal kar yahan charger laga hai... Arsalan ka!
ARSALAN: Ada, battery bas paanch percent thi... match bhi to bachana tha!
MEHAK (JOKE): Match bachate bachate, poore ghar ka WiFi gaya!
ABBA: Babaa, har cheez ko uski jagah pe rakho, to masla hi nahi banta.
ARSALAN: Hanji Abba. Aaj se charger apne kamre mein!
DANIYAL (PUNCHLINE): Case closed! Adalat barkhast!

ENDING: Har cheez apni jagah rakho, masla khud hi khatam!
