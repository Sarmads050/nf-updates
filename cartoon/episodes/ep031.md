## Kitchen • Subah

AMMI: Achha? Aur exam mein yeh science yaad kyun nahi rehti?   <-- JOKE
ARSALAN: Bas ek paratha aur, Ammi! Phir pakka bas!

AMMI: Main ne pehle hi kaha tha! Yeh paratha Imtiaz ka hai. Jao, Ada ko de kar aao.

## Drawing Room • Subah
IMTIAZ: Abba, office ki van das minute mein aa jayegi. Naashta aa raha hai?
ABBA: Humare zamane mein hum paanch minute mein naashta kar ke nikal jate thay!
ARSALAN: Ada, yeh lijiye... aakhri paratha. Bohot pyaar se laaya hoon.
IMTIAZ: Hao hao! Shukriya, Arsalan.
IMTIAZ: Lekin... is paratha ka aadha chand kaise ban gaya?
ARSALAN: Woh... raaste mein thanda na ho jaye, is liye check kiya tha!   <-- JOKE
ABBA: Arsalan! Bare bhai ka naashta raaste mein check hota hai?
IMTIAZ: Mei wenti, Abba, koi baat nahi. Arsalan, yeh tum kha lo.
IMTIAZ: Main office mein chai aur biscuit le loonga.
ARSALAN: Waah Ada! Aap duniya ke sab se ache bhai hain!
ABBA: Aur kal subah? Kal kya chautha paratha bhi check hoga?   <-- JOKE

## Kitchen • Raat
NARRATOR: Raat ko Ammi ne Arsalan ke liye ek khaas kaam rakha...
AMMI: Abba naya aata laaye hain. Kal ka aata aaj raat tum goondhoge!

MEHAK: Jo zyada khaye, woh zyada goondhe. Yeh bhi science hai, bhai!
ARSALAN: Theek hai... lekin kal pehla paratha mera hoga, pakka?
