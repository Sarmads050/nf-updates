TITLE: Ludo Ka Mukadma!
DURATION: ~110 s
HOOK: Narrator: Abba ne ghar mein ek naya qanoon banaya hai!
CHARACTERS: Abba, Ammi, Daniyal, Mehak, Hashir (toddler)
LOCATION: Nawabshah drawing room (night), kitchen (night)

SCENE 1: Drawing Room • Raat
- NARRATOR: Nawabshah ki thandi Itwaar raat... aur aaj Abba ne ghar mein ek naya qanoon banaya hai!
- ABBA: Aaj raat koi phone nahi chalega! Aaj poora ghar ludo khelega!
- DANIYAL: Objection, Abba! Phone meri bunyadi zaroorat hai!
- ABBA: Humare zamane mein bunyadi zaroorat sirf roti, chai aur ludo thi!   [JOKE]
- AMMI: Daniyal beta, apna phone Abba ke hawale kar do.
- DANIYAL: Le lijiye, Abba. Aaj se main ek bebas insaan hoon.
- AMMI: Aur shart yeh hai: jo haarega, woh sab ke liye chai banayega!

SCENE 2: Drawing Room • Aadhe Ghante Baad
- NARRATOR: Aadhe ghante baad... Mehak jeet rahi thi, aur Abba ki gotiyan pit rahi thin!
- MEHAK: Chhakka! Abba, aap ki aakhri goti bhi kat gayi!
- ABBA: Yeh daana hi kharab hai! Humare zamane ke daane imaandar hotay thay!
- DANIYAL: Your Honour, haar Abba rahe hain aur ilzaam daane par!   [JOKE]
- AMMI: Chaliye, ab aap ki baari hai. Daana phenkiye.
- ABBA: Daana? Main ne to nahi dekha... shayad qaaleen mein gum gaya hai.
- MEHAK: Ammi, daana abhi to yahin tha! Ab gayab ho gaya hai!
- DANIYAL: Objection! Jurm ki jagah se saboot gayab hai. Yeh saazish hai!
- AMMI: Hashir beta, aap ne daana dekha hai? Chhota sa, safaid sa?
- HASHIR: Dada... jeb! Dada jeb!   [JOKE]
- ABBA: Woh... main to daane ko thand se bacha raha tha!   [JOKE]
- DANIYAL: Mulzim ne jurm qabool kar liya! Aaj ki chai Abba banayenge!

SCENE 3: Kitchen • Raat
- AMMI: Suniye! Shart yaad hai na? Chai mein elaichi zaroor daaliyega!
- MEHAK: Abba, aap haare nahi. Aap ne aaj sab ko ek saath bitha diya.
- ABBA: Sach kahun? Phone ke baghair yeh raat sab se achhi guzri.
- DANIYAL: To kal raat bhi ludo? Is dafa daana main sambhalunga!
- ABBA: Nahi! Daana ab Hashir sambhalega. Woh ghar ka sab se imaandar gawah hai!   [JOKE]

JOKE / PUNCHLINE: 'roti, chai aur ludo'; 'haar Abba rahe hain, ilzaam daane par'; Hashir: 'Dada jeb!'; 'daane ko thand se bacha raha tha'; 'Hashir sab se imaandar gawah'.
ENDING: Abba loses the bet, makes chai and admits the phone-free night was the best. Lesson: Phone rakho, saath baitho — yehi asli jeet hai!

CAPTION: Itwaar raat, phone band, ludo shuru! 🎲😂 Abba haar rahe thay... phir daana hi gayab ho gaya! Asli gawah nikla 2 saal ka Hashir. Aap ke ghar mein ludo mein sab se zyada beimani kaun karta hai? Comment mein batayein!
