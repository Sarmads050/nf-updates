TITLE: Do Tamatar!
DURATION: ~110 s
HOOK: Narrator: Is ghar mein aaj kal sab se qeemti cheez sona nahi... tamatar hai!
CHARACTERS: Ammi, Sana (Bhabi), Abba, Daniyal, Hamza (toddler)
LOCATION: Nawabshah kitchen (dopahar), aangan, drawing room (raat)

SCENE 1: Kitchen • Dopahar
- NARRATOR: Nawabshah ke is ghar mein aaj kal sab se qeemti cheez sona nahi... tamatar hai!
- AMMI: Sana, gas aa gayi hai! Jaldi salan chadhao, warna gas phir chali jayegi.
- SANA: Ammi, masala tayyar hai, lekin tamatar to ek bhi nahi hai.
- ABBA (enters with thaila): Fikar na karo! Main bazaar se tamatar le aaya hoon.
- AMMI: Thaila to bilkul halka hai. Kitne laaye hain?
- ABBA (gives thaila): Poore do! Dukandar ne aise diye, jaise shaadi ka tohfa ho!   [JOKE]
- ABBA: Humare zamane mein tamatar tokri bhar ke aate thay, koi ginta bhi nahi tha!
- AMMI: Aur aaj kal gin kar aate hain. Do tamatar mein poore ghar ka salan kaise banega?
- SANA: Ammi, mere paas ek tarkeeb hai. Bas thora intezar kijiye!

SCENE 2: Aangan • Thori Der Baad
- NARRATOR: Lekin thori der baad... tamatar wala thaila hi gayab ho gaya!
- AMMI: Daniyal! Tamatar ka thaila dekha hai? Kitchen se gayab hai!
- DANIYAL: Objection! Ghar ki sab se qeemti cheez gayab? Main abhi tafteesh karta hoon!
- HAMZA (holding the thaila): Mela... bawl!
- DANIYAL: Mulzim mil gaya, Ammi! Lekin umar do saal hai... zamanat ho jayegi!   [JOKE]
- AMMI: Hamza, meri jaan, yeh ball nahi hai. Yeh to aaj ka salan hai!
- HAMZA (gives thaila): Lo... Dadi!
- SANA: Ammi, tarkeeb yeh hai: salan mein dahi aur thori imli daal dete hain.
- SANA: Khatta-pan bhi aa jayega, aur yeh do tamatar kal ke liye bach jayenge.
- AMMI: Waah Sana! Yeh hoti hai M.A. pass bahu ki samajh!

SCENE 3: Drawing Room • Raat Ka Khana
- ABBA: Waah! Aaj salan mein tamatar ka maza hi kuch aur hai!
- AMMI: Suniye, is mein ek bhi tamatar nahi hai. Yeh Sana ka dahi aur imli wala salan hai!
- ABBA: Kamaal hai! Humare zamane mein bhi itni samajhdar bahu nahi thi!
- ABBA: To phir mere laaye hue do tamatar kahan hain?
- DANIYAL: Fridge mein, Abba. Hamza ki pahunch se door... high security mein!   [JOKE / PUNCHLINE]

ENDING: Mehngai mein ghabrao nahi, samajhdari se kaam chalao!
