TITLE: Dholki Ki Practice!
DURATION: ~115 s
HOOK: MEHAK — Bhabi! Rabia aapi ki mehndi mein sirf saat din reh gaye hain!
CHARACTERS: MEHAK, SANA, ABBA, DANIYAL, HASHIR, AMMI (offscreen)
LOCATION: Drawing room (subah), Aangan (shaam)

SCENES:

## Scene 1 — Drawing Room • Subah
MEHAK: Bhabi! Rabia aapi ki mehndi mein sirf saat din reh gaye hain!
NARRATOR: Shaadiyon ka mausam aa gaya hai... aur is ghar mein dholki ki practice shuru hone wali hai!
SANA: Adi, sukoon se. Gaane hum aaj shaam aangan mein yaad karenge.
MEHAK: Lekin Bhabi, hamare ghar mein to dholak hi nahi hai!
ABBA: Achha hai. Dholak hogi to shor hoga, aur main akhbar nahi parh sakunga.
SANA: Abba, shaadi wala ghar hai. Thora shor to banta hai!
ABBA: Humare zamane mein ek dholak poore mohalle ki shaadiyon ke liye kaafi hoti thi!
MEHAK: Aur woh dholak aap hi bajate thay na, Abba? Dadi ne bataya tha!   <-- JOKE
ABBA: Woh... woh purani baat hai. Main akhbar parh raha hoon.

## Scene 2 — Aangan • Shaam
DANIYAL: Mehndi ki practice? Main taaliyan bajaunga, full volume mein!
SANA: Chalo, sab mere saath: Mehndi hai, raunaq hai, Rabia ki shaadi hai!
MEHAK: Bhabi, taaliyan to hain... lekin dhol ki taal ke bina maza nahi aa raha.
HASHIR: Dhum! Dhum! Dhum-dhum!
DANIYAL: Arre waah! Hashir ki steel ki plate to bilkul dholak ban gayi!
MEHAK: Bhabi, hamara dholak wala mil gaya... sirf do saal ka!   <-- JOKE
ABBA: Yeh shor kaun macha raha hai? Main khabarnama sun raha hoon!
ABBA: Dekho, taal hi ghalat hai! Plate aise nahi bajate.
DANIYAL: Objection, my lord! Abba ko taal ka itna pata kaise hai?
MEHAK: Kyunki Dadi kehti thin, Abba mohalle ke sab se achhe dholak wale thay!
HASHIR: Dada... lo!
ABBA: Achha, ek dafa dekho. Taal aise hoti hai... dha-dhin, dha-dhin!
SANA: Waah Abba! Ab lag raha hai shaadi wala ghar!
DANIYAL: Lekin Abba, andar aap ka khabarnama to chal raha hai!
ABBA: Khabar-wabar baad mein. Is waqt Rabia ki mehndi sab se bari khabar hai!   <-- JOKE
AMMI: Main ne pehle hi kaha tha! Sab se zyada shor yahi karenge!   <-- JOKE
SANA: Shaadi ki asal raunaq... ghar walon ki hansi hai.

JOKE: Mehak — 'hamara dholak wala mil gaya... sirf do saal ka!'; Abba — 'Rabia ki mehndi sab se bari khabar hai!'; Ammi — 'Main ne pehle hi kaha tha!'
ENDING: Abba who wanted quiet plays the plate-dholak loudest; Sana: shaadi ki asal raunaq ghar walon ki hansi hai.
SAFETY: no religion, army, politics or real names; Hashir is safe with Bhabi beside him (steel plate only).

CAPTION: Mehndi mein 7 din baaqi aur ghar mein dholak hi nahi 🥁😂 Phir 2 saal ke Hashir ki steel ki plate dholak ban gayi... aur jo Abba shor se tang thay, wohi sab se zyada bajane lage! Apne ghar ke us bande ko tag karein jo shaadi ki dholki mein sab se aage hota hai!
