TITLE: Abba Ka Voice Note!
DURATION: ~115 s
HOOK: Narrator — Abba ek naya kaam seekh rahe hain... WhatsApp voice note!
CHARACTERS: ABBA, FARAZ, MEHAK, AMMI, NIMRA (phone)
LOCATION: Drawing room (subah), Drawing room (das minute baad), Aangan (shaam)

SCENES:

## Scene 1 — Drawing Room • Subah
NARRATOR: Nawabshah ke is ghar mein aaj Abba ek naya kaam seekh rahe hain... WhatsApp voice note!
AMMI: Mehak ke Abba! Doodh chulhe par hai, dekhte rehna. Main chhat par kapre daal rahi hoon!
ABBA: Haan haan, dekh raha hoon!
ABBA: Faraz, yeh mic wala button dabaun, to awaaz Nimra tak chali jayegi?
FARAZ: Ji Abba. Button daba kar rakhein, bolein, phir chhor dein. Bas!
ABBA: Humare zamane mein chitthi likhte thay, ek hafte mein pohanchti thi!
MEHAK: Aur ab Abba ki chitthi ek second mein pohanchegi! Bolein, Abba!
ABBA: Nimra beta, kaisi ho? Yahan thand barh gayi hai...
MEHAK: Abba! Aap ne button pehle hi chhor diya. Sirf "Nimra" gaya hai!  [JOKE]
FARAZ: Abba, ungli mat uthaiyega. Jab tak baat khatam na ho!

## Scene 2 — Drawing Room • Das Minute Baad
NARRATOR: Das minute baad... Abba ne button aisa dabaya, ke chhora hi nahi!
ABBA: ...aur Nimra, moze pehna karo, garam paani piya karo, aur raat ko jaldi soya karo...
AMMI: Mehak ke Abba! Doodh ubal kar gir gaya! Aap kya kar rahe hain?
ABBA: Shh! Begum, aahista! Main voice note bana raha hoon!
AMMI: Voice note hai ya poori taqreer? Chulha bhi saaf aap hi karenge!
FARAZ: Abba... Ammi ki daant bhi voice note mein record ho gayi!  [JOKE]
ABBA: Arre! Ab kya karun? Mita do, jaldi!
MEHAK: Abba ne ungli utha di... aur das minute ka voice note Karachi chala gaya!  [JOKE]

## Scene 3 — Aangan • Shaam
NARRATOR: Shaam ko aangan mein... Karachi se Nimra ka jawab aa gaya!
NIMRA: Abba, aap ki das minute ki naseehat suni... aur Ammi ki daant bhi!
AMMI: Dekha? Ab poore Karachi ko pata hai ke doodh ubal gaya!
ABBA: Theek hai! Agli dafa chhota voice note... sirf paanch minute ka!  [JOKE]
MEHAK: Abba, chhota matlab... ek minute!
NIMRA: Lekin Abba, aap ki awaaz sun kar dil khush ho gaya. Roz bhejiyega!
AMMI: Baatein roz bhejiye... lekin pehle chulha band kar ke!  [JOKE]

JOKE: Pehli dafa sirf 'Nimra' gaya / Ammi ki daant bhi record ho gayi / ungli uthi aur 10 minute ka note Karachi chala gaya / 'chhota = sirf paanch minute'.
ENDING: Nimra: aap ki awaaz sun kar dil khush ho gaya — Ammi: baatein roz bhejiye, pehle chulha band kar ke.

CAPTION: Abba ne pehli dafa WhatsApp voice note bheja 📱😂 Pehli dafa sirf "Nimra" gaya... doosri dafa 10 minute ki naseehat aur Ammi ki daant bhi Karachi pohanch gayi! Aap ke ghar mein sab se lamba voice note kaun bhejta hai? Comment mein batayein!
