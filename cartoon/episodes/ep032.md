TITLE: Nayi Razai Kis Ki?
DURATION: ~115 s
HOOK: DANIYAL — Objection, my lord! Yeh nayi razai mere naam par hai!
CHARACTERS: DANIYAL, ARSALAN, AMMI, ABBA
LOCATION: Boys' bedroom (raat), Drawing room (agli raat)

SCENES:

## Bedroom • Raat
DANIYAL: Objection, my lord! Yeh nayi razai mere naam par hai!
NARRATOR: Nawabshah ki raatein thandi ho gayi hain... aur ghar mein nayi razai sirf ek hai!
ARSALAN: Daniyal bhai, main pehle bed par leta tha. Pehle aao, pehle pao!
DANIYAL: Yeh qabza gair-qanooni hai! Main subah adalat jaunga!
ARSALAN: Adalat subah khulegi na? Tab tak main so jata hoon!   <-- JOKE
AMMI: Kya shor macha rakha hai? Poora mohalla jaag jayega!
DANIYAL: Ammi, mujhe insaaf chahiye! Razai ka faisla kijiye!
AMMI: Aaj Arsalan ki baari, kal Daniyal ki. Aur kal main doosri razai bhi le aaungi.
DANIYAL: Theek hai... lekin kal yeh razai meri hai, sirf meri!
ARSALAN: Aaj aap purani razai lein, bhai... us mein muft ka AC laga hai!   <-- JOKE

## Drawing Room • Agli Raat
NARRATOR: Agli raat... Daniyal ki baari. Lekin nayi razai gayab hai!
DANIYAL: Arsalan! Meri razai kahan chhupayi? Sach batao!
ARSALAN: Sach mein bhai, maine nahi li! Subah Ammi ne usay dhoop mein daala tha.
DANIYAL: Ruko... sofa par yeh kaun so raha hai?
ARSALAN: Yeh to Abba hain... aur hamari nayi razai mein!
DANIYAL: Abba! Yeh to daaka hai! Objection!   <-- JOKE
ABBA: Kya hua? Dhoop mein nayi razai pari thi... main samjha meri hai!
ABBA: Aur humare zamane mein nayi cheez pehle ghar ke bare ko milti thi!
DANIYAL: Abba, yeh case ab bari adalat mein jayega!
ABBA: Bari adalat abhi kitchen mein hai. Bula lo!   <-- JOKE
AMMI: Main ne pehle hi kaha tha! Doosri nayi razai shaam ko aa gayi... tumhari almari mein hai!
DANIYAL: Almari mein? Aur hum poore ghar mein dhoond rahe thay!
ARSALAN: Bhai, aap ne Ammi ki baat suni hi nahi... objection overruled!   <-- JOKE
AMMI: Yeh razai Abba ki. Aur almari wali tum dono ki... mil kar odhna!
DANIYAL: Theek hai... lekin razai ke beech mein line khinchegi!
ABBA: Humare zamane mein chaar bhai ek razai mein sote thay... aur chup rehte thay!   <-- JOKE

JOKE: 'Adalat subah khulegi, tab tak main so jata hoon'; 'muft ka AC'; 'Bari adalat abhi kitchen mein hai'; 'objection overruled'; Abba's chaar bhai ek razai.
ENDING: Abba keeps the first razai, boys share the second one from their almari — mil kar odhna.
