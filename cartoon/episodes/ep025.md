TITLE: Dhund Ya Chashma?
DURATION: ~110 s
HOOK: Narrator: Nawabshah mein aaj subah itni dhund hai, ke saamne ka gate bhi nazar nahi aa raha!
CHARACTERS: Abba, Ammi, Arsalan, Faraz, Mehak
LOCATION: Nawabshah aangan (subah), drawing room, aangan (das baje)

SCENE 1: Aangan • Dhund Wali Subah
- NARRATOR: Nawabshah mein aaj subah itni dhund hai, ke saamne ka gate bhi nazar nahi aa raha!
- ARSALAN: Abba, itni dhund mein school jaana khatarnaak hai! Aaj chhutti kar loon?
- ABBA: Humare zamane mein hum dhund mein bhi cycle par time se school pohanchte thay!
- ARSALAN: Abba, aap ke zamane mein to dhund bhi aap se darti hogi!   [JOKE]
- AMMI: Bas! Dhund chhatne do. Van der se aayegi, lekin aayegi zaroor!
- ABBA: Aur Faraz se kehna, aaj bike dheere chalaye aur light jala kar rakhe.

SCENE 2: Drawing Room • Subah
- NARRATOR: Idhar drawing room mein... Faraz ne garam garam chai ka pehla ghoont liya.
- FARAZ: Arre! Sab kuch safaid ho gaya! Dhund to ghar ke andar aa gayi!
- MEHAK: Kaun si dhund, Ada Faraz? Kamre mein to sab saaf nazar aa raha hai.
- FARAZ: Mehak, mujhe to tum bhi ek safaid baadal jaisi lag rahi ho!   [JOKE]
- AMMI (enters): Kya hua? Subah subah kis ne shor machaya hai?
- FARAZ: Ammi, meri aankhon ko kuch ho gaya hai. Lagta hai doctor ke paas jaana parega!
- MEHAK: Ammi, in ke chashme par garam chai ki bhaap jam gayi hai!   [JOKE]
- AMMI: Health Department mein kaam karte ho, aur chashma saaf karna nahi aata?
- FARAZ: Ji Ammi... thand mein garam bhaap se sheesha dhundla ho jata hai.
- FARAZ: Lo, ab sab saaf hai! Aur Mehak bhi wapas Mehak lag rahi hai!   [JOKE]

SCENE 3: Aangan • Das Baje
- NARRATOR: Das baje tak... dhund bhi chhat gayi, aur Arsalan ki umeed bhi.
- ARSALAN: Ammi, dhund to chali gayi, lekin meri neend abhi tak nahi gayi!
- AMMI: Neend van mein poori kar lena. Van gate par khari hai!
- ABBA: Beta, dhund mein ehtiyat zaroori hai, lekin bahana nahi.
- ARSALAN: Theek hai Abba. Lekin kal ke liye thori zyada dhund mangwa dijiye ga!   [JOKE / PUNCHLINE]
- AMMI: Dhund nahi... kal tumhare liye do alarm lagaungi!

ENDING: Dhund mein ehtiyat karo, bahane nahi!
