TITLE: Bachon Ka Din!
DURATION: ~110 s
HOOK: Narrator — aaj bachon ka aalmi din, Arsalan ki maangon ki list!
CHARACTERS: ABBA, ARSALAN, DANIYAL, AMMI, HAMZA
LOCATION: Drawing room (shaam), aangan (shaam), drawing room (raat)

SCENES:

## Scene 1 — Drawing Room • Shaam
NARRATOR: Aaj 20 November hai, bachon ka aalmi din! Aur Arsalan ne maangon ki list bana li hai.
ARSALAN: Abba! Aaj bachon ka din hai. Meri maangein suniye!
ABBA: Achha? Suna do, beta. Main akhbar rakh deta hoon.
ARSALAN: Pehli maang: aaj raat koi homework nahi. Doosri: pocket money double!
ABBA: Humare zamane mein bachon ka din nahi hota tha... poora saal Abba ka din hota tha!  [JOKE]
ARSALAN: Ada Daniyal! Jaldi aayein, mujhe vakeel chahiye!
DANIYAL: Haazir hoon! Mera muakkil sirf apna haq maang raha hai, my lord!
ABBA: Theek hai. Aangan mein chalo, faisla Ammi ke saamne hoga.

## Scene 2 — Aangan • Shaam
NARRATOR: Aangan mein adalat lag gayi. Aur judge ki kursi par... Ammi!
AMMI: Haq ke saath zimmedari bhi hoti hai. Kal ka homework kiya, Arsalan?
ARSALAN: Ammi... woh aadha ho gaya hai... matlab pehla page.  [JOKE]
DANIYAL: Objection! Mera muakkil aadhe kaam ko bhi poora maanta hai!
AMMI: To phir aadhi pocket money ko bhi poora maan lo!  [JOKE]
ARSALAN: Yeh na-insaafi hai! Aaj bachon ka din hai, faisla koi bacha kare!
ABBA: Manzoor! Ghar ka sab se chhota bacha faisla karega... Hamza!
DANIYAL: Hamza to do saal ka hai, woh hamari baat maanega. Jeet pakki!

## Scene 3 — Drawing Room • Raat
NARRATOR: Raat ko adalat drawing room mein lagi. Judge sahab ke haath mein Arsalan ki homework copy thi!
DANIYAL: Judge sahab! Bhai ko mobile chahiye ya homework? Bataiye!
HAMZA: Bhai... pa-ho!  [JOKE]
ARSALAN: Mera vakeel bhi haar gaya... aur judge bhi Ammi ki taraf hai!
DANIYAL: Main pehla vakeel hoon jo do saal ke judge se case haar gaya!  [JOKE]
ABBA: Beta, haq bhi milega aur pyaar bhi. Bas pehle apni zimmedari.
AMMI: Homework khatam karo, phir sab ke saath ek ghanta cartoon dekhenge!
ARSALAN: Deal pakki! Aur agle saal mera vakeel... Hamza hoga!  [JOKE]

JOKE: Hamza (judge) gives Arsalan the homework copy: 'Bhai... pa-ho!' / Daniyal: pehla vakeel jo 2 saal ke judge se case haar gaya.
ENDING: Haq bhi, zimmedari bhi — homework ke baad sab ke saath cartoon.
