HOOK: Daniyal: 'Bhabi! Thand se haath jam gaye hain... chai ki appeal hai!'
CHARACTERS: DANIYAL, SANA, ABBA, AMMI, MEHAK
LOCATION: Nawabshah drawing room + kitchen (evening)

SCENES:
Scene 1 - Drawing Room • Shaam
1. DANIYAL: Bhabi! Thand se haath jam gaye hain... chai ki appeal hai!
2. NARRATOR: Nawabshah ki thandi shaam... aur har ghar ki tarah yahan bhi chai ka order shuru!
3. SANA: Theek hai! Sab batayein, kis ko kaisi chai chahiye?
4. ABBA: Meri kadak, double patti. Humare zamane mein chai kadak hi hoti thi!
5. AMMI: Meri chai pheeki hogi, Sana. Cheeni bilkul nahi daalna.
6. DANIYAL: Aur meri mein teen chamach cheeni... yeh meri final demand hai!
7. MEHAK: Aur meri aadha doodh, aadhi chai... aur upar malai nahi!
8. SANA: Waah! Main chai bana rahi hoon ya hotel khol rahi hoon?   <-- JOKE
Scene 2 - Kitchen • Shaam
9. NARRATOR: Kitchen mein Bhabi ne chaar cup aur chaar alag farmaishein tayyar kar dein...
10. SANA: Mehak, tray mein pehla cup Abba ka, doosra Ammi ka, teesra Daniyal ka.
11. MEHAK: Aur chautha mera! Bhabi, tray main le jaati hoon.
12. SANA: Dhyan se! Tray ghumana nahi, warna sab ka order badal jayega!
13. MEHAK: Fikar na karein, Bhabi... main expert waiter hoon!
Scene 3 - Drawing Room • Chai Time
14. MEHAK: Chai haazir hai! Garma garam, sab apna apna cup lijiye!
15. ABBA: Yeh kya? Itni meethi! Yeh to Daniyal ki teen chamach wali hai!
16. AMMI: Aur meri chai itni kadak... aaj to raat bhar neend nahi aayegi!
17. DANIYAL: Aur meri bilkul pheeki! Objection, my lord, yeh sazish hai!
18. MEHAK: Woh... darwaze se nikalte waqt tray thori si ghoom gayi thi.
19. SANA: Koi baat nahi! Sab apna cup badal lein, masla hal.
20. ABBA: Nahi nahi! Humare zamane mein jo chai mil jaye, chupchaap pee lete thay!
21. AMMI: Aur khaas taur par jab us mein teen chamach cheeni ho!   <-- JOKE
22. DANIYAL: To phir meri cheeni ka muawza kaun dega, my lord?
23. SANA: Main tumhare liye nayi chai bana deti hoon. Adi, sukoon se!
24. MEHAK: Aur is dafa tray Daniyal Bhai khud le kar aayenge!   <-- JOKE
25. AMMI: Shukriya, Sana. Thandi shaam mein tumhari chai hi asal razai hai.

JOKE: Abba refuses to swap cups ('humare zamane mein jo chai mil jaye pee lete thay') - Ammi: 'khaas taur par jab us mein teen chamach cheeni ho!'
ENDING: Sana makes Daniyal a fresh cup; Ammi thanks Sana - 'tumhari chai hi asal razai hai'. Lesson: Chai jaisi bhi ho, banane wale ka shukriya!
