TITLE: Ek Balti Ka Challenge!
DURATION: ~110 s
HOOK: AMMI — Faraz! Tanki khaali hai! Nalke se ek boond bhi nahi aa rahi!
CHARACTERS: AMMI, FARAZ, IMTIAZ, SANA, ABBA
LOCATION: Aangan (subah), Drawing room (subah), Aangan (shaam)

SCENES:

## Aangan • Subah
AMMI: Faraz! Tanki khaali hai! Nalke se ek boond bhi nahi aa rahi!
NARRATOR: Nawabshah mein aaj subah... ghar ki tanki khaali hai, aur sab ko jaldi hai!
FARAZ: Ammi, restart kiya tha? Matlab... motor ka button dabaya tha?
AMMI: Dabaya tha! Motor ghoon ghoon karti hai, lekin paani nahi aata!
FARAZ: Phir masla motor ka nahi hai. Line mein paani hi nahi aaya!
FARAZ: Khaali pipe se motor sirf hawa kheenchti hai, Ammi.
AMMI: Ghar mein har cheez tum theek karte ho. To paani bhi tum hi lao!
FARAZ: Ammi, main Health Department mein hoon... Water Board mein nahi!   <-- JOKE
AMMI: Main ne pehle hi kaha tha, raat ko baltiyan bhar kar rakho!

## Drawing Room • Subah
NARRATOR: Udhar Ada Imtiaz ko office ke liye der ho rahi thi...
IMTIAZ: Sana! Mujhe nahana hai, aur office ki van saat baje aayegi!
SANA: Adi, sukoon se. Main ne kal raat ek balti bhar kar rakhi thi.
IMTIAZ: Sirf ek balti? Is mein main poora kaise nahaunga?
ABBA: Humare zamane mein ek balti mein naha bhi lete thay, aur kapre bhi dho lete thay!
IMTIAZ: Hao hao, Abba... hamari company mein ise kehte hain 'mei you shui'... yaani paani nahi hai!   <-- JOKE
SANA: Mug se aahista aahista daalein... ek balti kaafi hogi.
IMTIAZ: Theek hai! Aaj ek balti ka challenge shuru!

## Aangan • Shaam
FARAZ: Ammi! Line mein paani aa gaya! Main abhi motor chalata hoon.
AMMI: Sab sun lo! Aaj raat saari baltiyan bhar kar rakhni hain!
SANA: Aur nahate waqt balti aur mug... is se paani bhi bachega.
IMTIAZ: Ammi, main ne aaj ek balti mein naha kar record bana diya!
ABBA: Waah! Ab tanki bhar gayi hai... aaj to main do balti se nahaunga!
SANA: Abba... humare zamane mein to ek balti mein naha lete thay na?   <-- JOKE
ABBA: Hmm... theek hai, ek balti. Lekin garam paani wali!   <-- JOKE
AMMI: Paani ki har boond qeemti hai... aaj bachaoge, to kal kaam aayegi!

JOKE: Faraz 'Health Department mein hoon, Water Board mein nahi'; Imtiaz 'mei you shui'; Sana turns Abba's 'humare zamane mein' back on him.
ENDING: Ammi — paani ki har boond qeemti hai; buckets filled at night.

CAPTION: Subah tanki khaali, sab ko office aur school ki jaldi... aur Abba ka 'humare zamane mein ek balti' 😂🪣 Apne ghar ke us bande ko tag karein jo sab se lamba nahata hai!
