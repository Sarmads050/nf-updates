# Earning plan — Sindhi Ghar Ki Kahani (research 2026-11-21)

## Platform earning from Pakistan
- **YouTube (main source).** Partner Program: 1,000 subscribers + (4,000 watch hours in 12 months OR 10M Shorts views in 90 days).
  The 500-subscriber tier is NOT available in Pakistan. From 1 Feb 2027 Shorts ad revenue also needs 10M Shorts views / 90 days,
  so for a growing channel **long videos (weekly 8+ min compilations) are the realistic route** (mid-roll ads at 8+ min, watch hours).
  Payout: AdSense wire transfer to a Pakistani bank, or Payoneer (Meezan Bank withdrawal).
  Sources: support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240, /12504220, /14728152, /6175006
- **Facebook:** in-stream/Reels ad earnings were stopped for Pakistani pages in March 2025; Content Monetization is invite-only.
  Use Facebook for reach + Stars, not as main income. (tribune.com.pk/story/2534763, propakistani.pk 2025/03/17)
- **Instagram / TikTok:** no Reels bonus, gifts, subscriptions or Creator Rewards for Pakistan — they are for **brand deals**.
- **Made for kids: NO.** Family comedy for everyone (FTC + YouTube guidance). "Made for kids" removes comments, personalised ads,
  memberships, end screens — less money and less reach.
- Rough Pakistan RPM (weak data): long video ~$0.3–1.5 per 1,000 views; Shorts ~Rs 8–25 per 1,000. Gulf/UK viewers pay more.

## Sponsorships
- Brands that already use cartoons in Pakistan: Olper's (own animated series), Safeguard (Commander Safeguard), Knorr, Bisconni.
  Good fits for a family show: tea, spices, milk, biscuits, telecom/fintech, food delivery, Daraz, and local Sindh shops
  (mithai, ajrak, cloth, schools/academies in Nawabshah/Hyderabad).
- Where: direct pitch (DM/email with media kit), Meta Creator Marketplace / branded content tools, Collabstr (Karachi),
  Karachi influencer agencies (e.g. Plan Z, Media Feathers, ExpoBird, Conversions Digital).
- Rough rates (weak data): 5k–15k followers = free product / a few thousand PKR; 15k–50k ≈ $30–150; 50k–200k ≈ $150–600 per post.
- Format: product inside the story (Ammi's chai, family orders food), branded festival episode, "brought to you by" end card.
  Always disclose: YouTube "paid promotion" box, Instagram/Facebook "Paid partnership" label. Fill `cartoon/SPONSOR.json`.
- Media kit: show concept, character sheet, followers/views/engagement per platform, audience city/age screenshots, ad formats + prices, sample integration, contact.

## Other income later
WhatsApp sticker pack of the family (free marketing first), stickers/mugs/Eid cards via Daraz/Instagram shop, Patreon (pays to Pakistan
via Payoneer), YouTube memberships (after Partner Program), licensing to Pakistani OTT (e.g. Tamasha) once there is a big catalogue.

## Roadmap
1. **0–1k:** post daily, "Not made for kids", original audio, weekly compilation, same characters every day, save analytics screenshots.
2. **1k–10k:** push to 4,000 watch hours via compilations → apply YPP, set up AdSense/Payoneer, make media kit, pitch local Sindh shops (barter/small fee).
3. **10k–100k:** branded episodes with mid-size brands, memberships/Patreon, sticker pack, contact Karachi agencies.
4. **100k+:** season sponsorship with telecom/FMCG, OTT licensing, merch, target Gulf/UK Sindhi diaspora.
