# Privacy Policy - Naushahro Feroze Updates

This app is used only by the owner of the "Naushahro Feroze Updates" Facebook Page to publish public news and job posts on that Page.

- The app does not collect, store or share any personal data of Facebook users.
- It only uses the Page owner's own Page access token to publish posts and read the Page's follower count.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Data deletion

The app stores no user data. To remove the app's access, the Page owner can remove the app from Facebook Settings > Business Integrations. For any question, send a message to the Naushahro Feroze Updates Facebook Page.
